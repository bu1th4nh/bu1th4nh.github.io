TIANA

A Training-Informed Agent for Nutrition and Affordability.

An LLM-agnostic agent for end-to-end post-exercise meal planning and grocery cart preparation.

Presented by Thanh Bui  ·  University of Central Florida  ·  Fall 2026 Press → to begin  ·  ? for controls
One session

  1. Garmin activity data and training load

  2. Personalized nutritional targets

  3. USDA food composition data and real-time Walmart products

  4. Constrained optimization of the meal and basket

  5. Verification, with up to three repair attempts

  6. A recovery meal and a reviewable shopping cart

01How it worksNine tools, one ReAct agent

The LLM orchestrates the tools, while deterministic code computes and checks every plan.

The LLM

Orchestrates and judges.

The LLM orchestrates the tools through an LLM-driven loop and judges products and labels.

Deterministic code

Computes the numbers.

Nutritional calculations, constrained optimization, and ordering constraints: the targets, quantities, and costs, over about 5.6 million meals in total.

The verifier

Checks every plan.

Nutritional adequacy, dietary compliance, budget feasibility, and product-label consistency, triggering up to three repair attempts when necessary.

Runs
120 of the author's runs from 2025-2026, 40 each of easy, medium, and hard runs
Models
Three self-hosted, open-weight models: Muse Glimmer 30B, Gemma 4 31B, Mistral Small 3
Exp. 1
Each model runs TIANA on two GPU types, 720 sessions in total
Exp. 2
The same models solve the same runs with web search only, graded by TIANA's check
Notably

The verifier rejects the first plan in almost every session, mainly because of items out of stock and discrepancies between USDA protein estimates and product nutrition labels.

OverallAn agentic architecture that orchestrates LLM-based reasoning, external tools, existing knowledge, and deterministic algorithms succeeds where conventional LLM prompting does not.
